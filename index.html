Important notes

Topic-overlap percentages are analytical estimates, not official equivalence scores, difficulty ratings or placement guarantees. The curriculum lists 167 academic credits plus 3 activity credits through 100 activity points. Minor and Honors are separate optional routes requiring 20 additional credits each. Micro-specialization is a separate elective-alignment option; consult the department for current availability and registration rules.
